## Qualification and session outline

Before booking, establish: what calls they want to handle; whether this is their business or a client's; their current tools; and whether they can spare 20 minutes this week. Do not request API keys, real caller records, or client secrets in a message.

During the session:

1. **Minutes 0–3:** Have them describe the call problem and what they use today.
2. **Minutes 3–12:** Watch them find model setup, choose a simple agent brief, and reach Test Agent. Let them try before offering help. Note every intervention.
3. **Minutes 12–17:** Run a fictional caller through the flow. Try a correction and an unavailable action; inspect the outcome together. If provider setup blocks testing, record the blocker instead of claiming activation.
4. **Minutes 17–20:** Ask what they expected, what confused them, and what would make them use it again. Invite a one-week pilot only if the use case fits.

Record signup completion, provider setup completion, successful call, minutes to first call, interventions, biggest blocker, and willingness to return. Get permission before recording a session. A positive comment is not a testimonial permission or buying commitment.
